Brief, self-dismissing presentations. They interrupt whatever is on the island and hand it back when they finish.

Persistent and interactive. Up to two run at once — the second one detaches as the minimal indicator.

  • TapRuns the primary action — play/pause, answer a call, stop a timer.
  • Touch & holdExpands the island into the detailed view. Hold again, tap outside, or swipe up to collapse.
  • Swipe ←/→Switches between two concurrent activities. The pill follows your finger and springs back.
  • Swipe ↓Expands from the compact state.
  • KeyboardEnter tap · Space expand · ←→ swap · Esc collapse.

The island is a real focusable control with aria-live announcements, so screen readers get every presentation change.
